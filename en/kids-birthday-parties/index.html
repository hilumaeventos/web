Kids' birthday parties in Manacor

A venue just for your party: the little ones playing in the indoor soft play and the grown-ups in the room, relaxed and able to keep an eye on them.

The soft play

Room to play big

Slides, a rope bridge, a climbing net, rings, soft blocks and padded flooring. An indoor play area for children aged 3 to 12, always with an adult from your group keeping watch.

Your way

Set up the party however you like

Bring whatever you fancy and set it up your own way:

  • Snacks, food and drinks
  • Cake and piñata
  • Decorations
  • Kitchen area with fridge, microwave, coffee machine and oven
Extra

How about a character?

We have character costumes so someone from your group can give everyone a surprise. They're not included in the venue price: you book them separately.
