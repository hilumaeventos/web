Frequently asked questions

The things you ask us most. If you can't find your answer, message us on WhatsApp.

Do we share the venue with other families?

No. During your booking, the venue is for the exclusive use of you and your guests.

Can we bring our own food and cake?

Yes. We don't provide catering, so you can bring whatever you like: food, drinks, cake, piñata and decorations. You'll have a fridge, microwave, coffee machine and oven.

How many people does it hold?

The venue's maximum capacity is 75 people.

What ages is the soft play for?

The soft play is for children aged 3 to 12. They must always be supervised by a responsible adult from your group.

Which days and times are you open?

Every day, Monday to Sunday, bank holidays included. There are three time slots: morning from 10:00 to 14:00, afternoon from 16:00 to 20:00 and evening from 20:30 to 23:30.

How do we book and how much is the deposit?

Pick a free time slot on the calendar and message us on WhatsApp. Your booking is confirmed with a 50 € deposit, which comes off the total.

What if we need to cancel?

You can cancel whenever you like, but the 50 € deposit is non-refundable.

Are the characters included?

No. Character costumes are a separate extra. You can add them to your party at Hiluma or hire them to take away; we'll tell you the price on WhatsApp.

How should we leave the venue when we finish?

There's no need for a deep clean, but please leave it tidy: put the rubbish in the bags we leave for you, clear away food and decorations, pick up anything that's fallen on the floor, wipe the tables with a cloth and put the chairs back in place.

All clear?
