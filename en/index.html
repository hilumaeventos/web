How it works

Booking takes a couple of minutes and there's no need to call.

  1. Pick a date

    Check the calendar to see which days and time slots are free: morning, afternoon or evening.

  2. Message us

    WhatsApp opens with your booking already written. We confirm it with a 50 € deposit, which comes off the total.

  3. Get ready

    Before the day, we'll send you directions, how to get in and the venue rules.

  4. Enjoy it

    When you finish, leave the room tidy, just as you found it, and head home relaxed.
